## What each illustration contains

Each SVG uses two panels:
- **Clinical illustration:** a simplified conceptual eye/test diagram with labels relevant to the lecture.
- **Equipment / test setup:** a schematic equipment panel labelled with the instrument or clinical tool used in that lesson.

These are original educational schematics, not clinical photographs, manufacturer-specific product images, or diagnostic images. They are intended to explain the concept and identify the relevant equipment category. Device photographs will only be added when a suitable, accurately identified and reusable source is verified.

## Rendering compatibility

The academy's `renderLectureMarkdown()` and `inlineFormat()` functions in `app.js` recognise HTTPS Markdown image links. The lesson image URLs point to the public repository's `main` branch under `assets/diagnostic-optometry/`.
