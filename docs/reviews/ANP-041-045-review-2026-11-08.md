## Corrections applied
1. ANP-042-L01: corrected the question about cavernous sinus contents. CN VI alone traverses the cavernous sinus proper adjacent to the internal carotid artery; CN III, CN IV, V1 and V2 are in the lateral wall. Updated its options and explanation without changing IDs or question count.
2. ANP-044-L01: corrected the presbyopia question to focus on age-related increased crystalline-lens stiffness and reduced accommodative amplitude, avoiding the misleading suggestion that ciliary muscle loss is the principal mechanism.
